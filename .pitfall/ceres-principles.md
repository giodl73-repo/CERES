# CERES Principles

## CERES-P-01: The Hypothesis Is Testable, Not Assumed

**Decision rule:** Treat local-production viability as a hypothesis with
registered falsifiers, not as a premise the catalog must rescue.

**Rationale:** A rigorous null result is useful; silent scope expansion to make
every trade look viable would destroy the evidence value.

**Test:** Research, playbooks, and pitch language preserve demand-collapse,
regulatory-capture, labor-regime, and supply-chain falsifiers.

**Evidence:** `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, and `specs/2026-04-18-ceres-design.md`.

## CERES-P-02: Catalog Schema Is The Simulation Contract

**Decision rule:** Keep catalog frontmatter schema-complete and explicit; new
fields require schema updates rather than silent entry-local invention.

**Rationale:** Tier A reads frontmatter directly, so malformed or invented fields
can create failed cells or silently wrong simulation evidence.

**Test:** Rust tests and schema docs preserve required fields, validation errors,
and RLINE facility projection boundaries.

**Evidence:** `catalog/SCHEMA.md`, `corpus/canon/PRINCIPLES.md`, and
`cargo test`.

## CERES-P-03: Estimates Are Research-Paper Level

**Decision rule:** Use cited order-of-magnitude ranges, uncertainty flags, and
cross-checks; do not present supplier quotes, real procurement plans, or
due-diligence-grade precision.

**Rationale:** CERES helps decide what is worth investigating further, not what
to buy or approve.

**Test:** Methodology, style guide, editorial roles, and catalog review require
citation, scope, numeracy, and precision honesty.

**Evidence:** `docs/METHODOLOGY.md`, `docs/STYLE-GUIDE.md`, and
`.roles/editorial/E-3-numeracy-checker.md`.

## CERES-P-04: Historical Function Before Aesthetic Reuse

**Decision rule:** Historical forms supply functional requirements and
constraints, not romantic decoration or convenient precedent.

**Rationale:** A forge, oven, loom, or artisan square only informs modern design
when its labor regime, supply chain, ownership form, and failure mode are named.

**Test:** Research files document functional requirements, historical forms,
decline verdicts, source limits, and cross-cultural divergence.

**Evidence:** `research/README.md`, `research/trades/smithing/DECLINE-VERDICT.md`,
`research/trades/baking/DECLINE-VERDICT.md`, and
`research/trades/weaving/DECLINE-VERDICT.md`.

## CERES-P-05: Reuse Uses Neutral Foundations, Not CERES Policy

**Decision rule:** Downstream systems may consume bounded evidence or neutral
SCENARIUM/RALLY/RLINE foundations, but they must not treat CERES catalog schema,
thresholds, or lens math as a generic economy or crafting contract.

**Rationale:** CERES economics are local-production research policy, not a
portable game or public-systems API.

**Test:** README reuse boundary and adoption docs separate RALLY evidence,
RLINE facility vocabulary, and CERES-owned market/cooperative/civic semantics.

**Evidence:** `README.md`, `docs/scenarium-adoption.md`, and
`simulations/tier-a-comparator/README.md`.
